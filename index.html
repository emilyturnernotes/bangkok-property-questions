What should I explain before the property search begins?

Start with your purpose: are you looking for a home to live in, a rental or a property you may eventually sell? Share your preferred locations, budget, timing and any requirements that would be difficult to compromise on.

Be specific about your daily routine. For example, a tenant who travels by train might prioritise access to a BTS or MRT station over extra living space. A buyer who works from home might value a quiet room, reliable layout and privacy more than proximity to nightlife.

Ask the agent which requirements are realistic together and where you may need to make a trade-off. This helps prevent a long list of viewings that do not meet your basic needs.

Which areas should I compare?

Ask the agent to suggest areas based on how you intend to use the property, not simply on their reputation. Prima Realty covers prime residential locations including Sukhumvit, Thonglor, Phrom Phong, Langsuan, Wireless Road, Sathorn and the Chao Phraya Riverside.

A buyer comparing Sathorn with a riverside property, for instance, might ask how each location fits their commute, preferred amenities and transport options. Request the actual route from a property rather than relying only on the neighbourhood name.

If possible, visit at different times of day. Note traffic, noise, pedestrian access and the distance to places you use regularly. What feels convenient during a quiet viewing may feel different on a weekday morning.

What is included in the property and the deal?

A polished viewing can make it easy to overlook practical details. Ask for a clear explanation of what is included in the proposed sale or lease, and which details still need confirmation.

For a rental, ask whether furniture, appliances and parking are included. Check the proposed lease length, deposit, payment schedule and conditions for renewal or early termination. If you have a pet, ask about the building’s rules and whether the specific landlord permits it.

For a purchase, ask which fixtures and furnishings are part of the offer, what documents are available and which costs each party is expected to pay. Do not assume that an item seen during a viewing will remain in the property. Request written confirmation before relying on it.

How should I compare luxury properties?

Ask how the type of property affects your day-to-day experience and what information is available about its management and rules.

For example, someone considering a branded residence might ask which services are included and which carry additional charges. A penthouse buyer might want to understand the usable outdoor space, privacy and maintenance responsibilities.

Compare properties against the same criteria: location, layout, building facilities, ongoing obligations and suitability for your intended use. Ask to review the relevant documents and verify any feature that is important to your decision.

What should I check before making an offer?

Ask what information you should have before committing and what the next steps would be if your offer is accepted. Clarify the proposed timeline, the documents needed and any conditions you want addressed.

For example, if you are buying from abroad, ask early what documentation may be relevant to your circumstances. Property ownership and transaction requirements can depend on the buyer and property type, so obtain independent legal advice rather than relying on assumptions.

For a lease, read the draft agreement carefully before paying or signing. Ask about repairs, responsibilities for utilities, inventory checks and how the deposit will be handled. If a term is unclear, request an explanation and, where appropriate, professional advice.

How can an agent help if I am selling or leasing my property?

If you own a residential property, ask how the agency approaches presenting it to prospective buyers or tenants. Prima Realty offers a list-your-property service as well as sales and leasing services.

Prepare details about the property’s layout, condition, furnishings, building rules and any documents you have. Ask what information the agency needs, how enquiries and viewings will be coordinated, and how you will receive feedback.

Before agreeing to representation, review the proposed terms. Clarify any fees, the duration of the arrangement and your responsibilities. Written terms help both sides understand what has been agreed.

How will we communicate during the search?

A useful working relationship depends on clear expectations. Ask how often you can expect updates, how viewings will be arranged and who will answer follow-up questions. Let the agent know your preferred contact method and any constraints on your availability.

You can also ask a Real estate agent in Bangkok how to provide feedback after a viewing. Instead of saying a property is simply unsuitable, explain whether the issue was its layout, location, condition or terms. That information can make the next selection more relevant.

Frequently asked questions

Can Prima Realty help with renting as well as buying?

Yes. The agency offers residential leasing and property sales. Explain whether you are looking to rent, buy, lease out or sell, so the conversation starts with the right objective.

Should I view a property before reviewing the terms?

You can use an initial viewing to decide whether a property interests you, but ask for the relevant terms before committing. For rentals, that includes the proposed lease conditions. For purchases, ask what documents and transaction details are available for review.

What if I am interested in a pet-friendly rental?

Tell the agency about your pet at the start, including any details a landlord or building may need. Ask for confirmation of both the building rules and the landlord’s conditions before making a commitment.

What if I am not ready to decide?

Ask what information would help you compare your options and what the next steps would involve. Take time to review documents, revisit your priorities and seek independent professional advice where needed. A viewing does not have to become an immediate decision.
